# Privacy Policy — Nova Publisher

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Nova Publisher is a personal tool run by Aakshant Kumar on his own computer. It posts to
**his own** LinkedIn profile through LinkedIn's official API. It is not a public service,
and no one else signs in to it.

## What it accesses

- **Your LinkedIn sign-in (OpenID Connect):** your member ID and name, so a post can name
  its author.
- **Posting permission (`w_member_social`):** used only to publish posts and images that
  the account owner has reviewed and approved one at a time.

It does not read your connections, messages, feed or other people's data.

## Where data is stored

- The access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file that is never
  committed to any repository or sent anywhere except to LinkedIn's own API.
- A local list of which projects have already been posted is kept on the same computer.
- Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

## Retention and deletion

Deleting the local token file removes access immediately. Access can also be revoked at
any time from LinkedIn: **Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services**.
